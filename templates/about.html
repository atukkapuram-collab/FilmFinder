ABOUT FILMFINDER

Better recommendations. Better discoveries.

FilmFinder combines multiple recommendation approaches to help you discover movies that match your interests. Behind the experience is a system built through experimentation, evaluation, and continuous refinement.

Explore the Technology

THE IDEA

Finding a movie should be the easy part.

With thousands of movies to choose from, finding something worth watching can be surprisingly difficult. FilmFinder was created to make that process more personal by using movie information and audience rating patterns to generate recommendations tailored to individual preferences.

THE RECOMMENDATION ENGINE

Three approaches. One smarter recommendation system.

No single recommendation method captures every dimension of what makes someone enjoy a movie. FilmFinder combines content analysis, semantic understanding, and collaborative filtering to produce a more personalized result.

01

Content-Based Filtering

Understanding what you like

FilmFinder analyzes movie genres, tags, and plot information to identify films with similar characteristics to the movies you enjoy.

TF-IDF 25% of hybrid weighting
02

Semantic Understanding

Looking beyond keywords

Sentence Transformer embeddings represent movie descriptions as numerical vectors. This allows FilmFinder to identify similarities in plot meaning and themes, even when movies use different words.

Sentence Transformers 5% of hybrid weighting
03

Collaborative Filtering

Learning from rating patterns

By examining how users with similar rating patterns evaluate movies, FilmFinder can identify films that someone may enjoy based on the preferences of other viewers.

User Similarity 70% of hybrid weighting

THE HYBRID APPROACH

Combining different perspectives into one result.

Each method produces its own ranking of potential recommendations. FilmFinder combines these rankings using Reciprocal Rank Fusion, allowing movies that rank highly across multiple methods to receive stronger combined scores.

The current optimized configuration gives the greatest weight to collaborative filtering while retaining content-based and semantic signals.

Collaborative Filtering 70%
Content-Based Filtering 25%
Semantic Understanding 5%

ENGINEERING & OPTIMIZATION

Built through testing, not guesswork.

Developing a recommendation system involves more than combining algorithms. FilmFinder's models were evaluated across different configurations to identify approaches that produced more relevant recommendations.

232 Hybrid configurations evaluated
6 Collaborative neighbor configurations tested
100 Sampled users in hybrid evaluation

EXPERIMENT 01

Optimizing the hybrid recommendation model

Different combinations of content-based, semantic, and collaborative recommendation weights were evaluated. The configuration producing the strongest validation NDCG@10 was selected for final testing.

On the held-out test sample, the optimized hybrid achieved a 0.0833 NDCG@10, compared with 0.0757 for collaborative filtering alone.

RELATIVE IMPROVEMENT +10.0% NDCG@10 compared with collaborative filtering
Hybrid 0.0833
Collaborative 0.0757

EXPERIMENT 02

Improving rating prediction accuracy

The collaborative filtering model was tested with six different neighbor counts. The configuration using 50 neighbors produced the lowest validation mean absolute error.

On its separate held-out test set, the model reduced prediction error compared with a baseline that simply predicts each user's average rating.

LOWER TEST MAE 7.95% Reduction compared with the user-average baseline
Collaborative 0.683
Baseline 0.742

Results are from separate MovieLens-based evaluation experiments using held-out data. Hybrid recommendation metrics and rating prediction metrics measure different aspects of model performance and should not be compared directly.
